About Old City Mongolia

Connecting travelers with authentic Mongolian experiences since 2010

Our Story

Old City Mongolia was born from a passion for sharing the beauty and culture of our homeland with the world. Founded in 2010, we started as a small family guesthouse in Ulaanbaatar's historic district.

What began as a dream to offer travelers authentic experiences has grown into a trusted name in Mongolian tourism. Our guesthouse has welcomed thousands of guests from every continent, each leaving with unforgettable memories and deeper appreciation for Mongolian culture.

Today, we combine traditional hospitality with modern comfort, carefully curating experiences that connect our guests with the heart and soul of Mongolia.
