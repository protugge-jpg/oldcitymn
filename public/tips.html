Travel Tips

Helpful pointers for a smooth and respectful journey through Mongolia.

Essential Know-How

These tips are placeholders that can be expanded with local insights, updated pricing, and seasonal guidance.

Money & Payments

  • Carry small bills for local markets and taxis.
  • ATMs are common in the city but limited in rural areas.
  • Ask about card acceptance before arriving at attractions.

Culture & Etiquette

  • Accept tea or snacks with your right hand as a sign of respect.
  • Ask permission before photographing people or sacred places.
  • Learn a few Mongolian greetings for warm interactions.

Weather & Packing

  • Layer clothing for big temperature swings day to night.
  • Pack wind protection for the steppe and desert regions.
  • Bring sunscreen and sunglasses year-round.

Travel Etiquette

  • Stay with your guide on open-range excursions.
  • Respect herding families' privacy and property boundaries.
  • Leave no trace when visiting nature sites.
