About Us – Old City Mongolia Experience

Old City Mongolia Experience was opened in May 2024, and since then we have had the joy of welcoming travelers from all around the world. What started as a small guesthouse has grown into a place where people meet, share stories, and discover the real spirit of Mongolia. Every guest who walks through our doors becomes part of our journey—and we are grateful for each traveler who chooses to stay with us.

We offer comfortable accommodation in the heart of Ulaanbaatar, and we also organize unforgettable trips across Mongolia. Since June 2025, we have been carefully planning and creating travel experiences that allow our guests to explore Mongolia’s vast landscapes, rich culture, and local way of life. By contacting us, we will personally help you plan your journey so you can experience Mongolia in a meaningful, safe, and memorable way.

We are proud to be located in the very center of the city, close to everything Ulaanbaatar has to offer. Beginning in June 2026, we will also host special summer events at the fountain in front of the State Department Store. These events were our own idea, created to bring travelers and local people together. In cooperation with the Ministry of Tourism and the Mayor of Ulaanbaatar, we have decided to make these events a yearly summer tradition.

At Old City Mongolia Experience, we believe that traveling is not just about visiting places—it is about connecting with people, culture, and stories. We look forward to welcoming you and helping you create memories that will stay with you long after you leave Mongolia.
