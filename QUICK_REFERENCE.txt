================================================================================
BOOKING WORKFLOW
================================================================================

ROOM BOOKING:
User clicks "Book Now"
    ↓
Selects room (Male/Female)
    ↓
Chooses dates
    ↓
Selects beds (1-4)
    ↓
Enters guest info (name, email, phone)
    ↓
Price calculated: nights × $25
    ↓
Clicks "Proceed to Payment"
    ↓
Enters payment details
    ↓
Receives confirmation
    ↓
Appears in admin dashboard

TRIP BOOKING:
User clicks "Book Trip"
    ↓
Selects trip (4 options)
    ↓
Chooses start date
    ↓
Selects participants
    ↓
Enters guest info (name, email, phone)
    ↓
Price calculated: trip price × participants
    ↓
Clicks "Proceed to Payment"
    ↓
Enters payment details
    ↓
Receives confirmation
    ↓
Appears in admin dashboard
