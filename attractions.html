Travel Tips

Simple guidance to help you plan, pack, and travel smoothly in Mongolia.

Plan With Confidence

Use these quick tips to prepare for your adventure. The information below follows the same design and layout as the rest of the site, making it easy to expand later with more local insights.

Quick Planning Checklist

  1. Season: Travel between mid-June and mid-October for the most reliable conditions.
  2. Connectivity: Buy a local SIM on arrival for maps and translations.
  3. Packing: Bring layers and a windproof jacket for fast-changing weather.
  4. Reservations: Confirm stays and tours in advance to avoid surprises.

On-the-Ground Advice

Currency

Carry Mongolian tugrik for daily payments. Exchange a portion on arrival.

Transportation

Plan extra time in Ulaanbaatar due to traffic and negotiate taxi fares early.

Rural Travel

Expect limited signal outside towns and pack offline maps where possible.

Respect & Etiquette

  • Ask permission before photographing people or sacred areas.
  • Accept offerings with your right hand as a sign of respect.
  • Leave no trace in open landscapes and protected areas.
